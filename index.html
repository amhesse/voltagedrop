Notes

  1. Resistance and reactance from NFPA 70 (NEC) Chapter 9, Table 9, copper conductors in steel conduit.
  2. Conduit is assumed metallic (worst-case reactance) with copper conductors.
  3. Effective Z = R·PF + X·sin(acos(PF)).
  4. Single-phase loads only.
  5. Segment length is doubled to include the return conductor.
  6. NEC informational notes recommend max 3% on a branch circuit and 5% overall including the feeder.
  7. Segment current is the sum of all downstream loads at the source voltage and entered power factor.
  8. Material pricing from Southwire market pricing, 11/16/2017. The 3/0 price is interpolated (source value was corrupt).
  9. Labor is cable only (no trenching or conduit), 2010 RSMeans.
  10. Cost is combined material and labor for both conductors.
  11. Ampacity from NEC Table 310.16, 75°C copper, with the small-conductor limits of 240.4(D) (#14: 15 A, #12: 20 A, #10: 30 A). No derating for ambient temperature or conductor fill is applied. With "continuous load" checked, current is multiplied by 1.25 before comparing.
  12. "Auto" picks the lowest-cost set of sizes that meets the drop target and ampacity; pick a size to override a segment.
